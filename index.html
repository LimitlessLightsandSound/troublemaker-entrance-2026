The look

Color, movement and a strong entrance.

A continuous sequence of rectangular portals creates depth along the guest approach. Coordinated lighting and projection carry the look from the tunnel to the architecture.

Included production

The complete entrance package.

Tunnel + front lighting
  • 18 portals, approximately 12′ wide × 10′ high, at nominal 4.5′ centers
  • 162 installed Titan tubes: three across each header and three on each side, with intentional gaps
  • 176 tubes supplied, including spares; Schedule 40 frames, bases and ballast
  • Two Pluto Fresnels on combo stands for the front of the tunnel
  • grandMA3 onPC control and LumenRadio Stardust wireless control
Projection + infrastructure
  • Four Epson 13,000-lumen projectors: two for the arch and two for the 54′ × 20′ wall
  • Lens package, truss towers and 24″ projector top plates
  • Media server, basic branded motion content and on-site mapping
  • Generator and fuel allowance, 120/208V distribution, Socapex, True1 and SDI cable
  • Cable crossovers, installation crew, lighting and projection operators, delivery and strike

A few site details to confirm.

projection

Measure the full arch face

The 15′ × 15′ dimension describes the opening only. Exterior dimensions, projector locations and sightlines determine final lens selection and coverage. Mapping is designed for after-dark viewing.

layout

Confirm the route and access

The base layout spans 76.5′ from first to last portal. Final centers, clear passage, ballast and weather protection will be coordinated on site. Guest flow remains subject to the venue’s approved access plan.

content

Build around your artwork

Basic branded motion content and mapping are included. Final logos, artwork and creative direction are needed before programming; elaborate custom animation can be scoped separately.
